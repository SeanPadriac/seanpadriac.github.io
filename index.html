Purpose & Mission

The core purpose of this site is to share the music I have created for people to enjoy, while simultaneously providing a dedicated platform to give back. I wanted to make a difference somehow and realized I could contribute through my art. Because of this, 50% of all licensing proceeds and digital download contributions are shared directly with the MS Society to support vital research and family resources.

So listen, enjoy, and donate what you can.
