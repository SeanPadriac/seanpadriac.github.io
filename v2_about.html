About the Artist

Sean padraic is a singer-songwriter of original compositions who has been playing guitar and piano for several years. For Sean, music is a major part of life that holds the power to heal, bring joy, and offer comfort. He writes simple, songs and melodies that are rooted in his personal experiences and the world around him. Drawing from a wide foundation of musical influences, the act of making music is not just a passion—it is simply an essential part of who he is.Sean's instruments of choice include the 6 and 12-string acoustic guitar, mandolin, slide guitar, harmonica, and piano.
